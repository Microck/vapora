# vapora 2.2.2

History loads in the background without opening a browser window or taking focus. Its button sits below Save settings and Load settings. The app uses History throughout its interface.

- Open several Details windows at once to compare profiles or captured records. Drag their title bars, move them with arrow keys, and close the focused window with Escape.
- Download an Obsidian vault ZIP from Exports. Profile notes use stable Steam IDs, name aliases and links for observed friendships. Coverage, missing observations and dated history stay visible. No plugin or new Steam request is needed.

The custom 2.2.1 portable launcher has been withdrawn. This release uses the original portable launcher and keeps data in `Vapora-data` beside the EXE. The launcher still needs enough free space in Windows TEMP to unpack its bundled runtime. Move the EXE and its data folder together; remembered keys remain tied to the OS account and machine that encrypted them.

History can be blocked by its provider; Retry and local import remain available, and Steam scans continue independently. Original captures and their identifiers remain unchanged.

Downloads are unsigned and not notarized. Verify them against `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Native package tests use local HTTP fixtures; the live background history check covers one account and does not establish large-network behavior.
